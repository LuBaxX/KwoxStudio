01 / MEMBERSHIP

A little extra.
About VIP.

VIP is a special program that offers exclusive benefits to our members.

✦

How it works through the franchise

In the Find The Piece franchise, your VIP will match on every games of this. However, the gamepass aren't replicated. That mean if you buy the VIP in one game (call game A) with gamepass, in the second game in the same franchise (call game B), you will have the VIP that you bought but not the gamepass. This is explained by the fact that the game, the gameplay and the gamedesign could not be exactly the same and the gamepass could be different or won't exist in the second game. For the VIP, even if the game isn't the same, KwoxStudio will make sure that the VIP is replicated in all the games of the franchise. If you have decided to cancel the VIP, it will be canceled in all the games of the franchise when your subscription ends. That's means you can cancel it at any time in any game of the franchise.

VIP gifted

About a gifted VIP, it works only in the specific games where you get it. We can also gifted ourselves VIPs free, and don't worry we won't ask you to pay at the end of the free try. The gifted is completly a Gifted. If you had a VIP and someone gifted you a better one, the gifted will replace your VIP and you will have the benefits of the gifted VIP. The gifted VIP is a special VIP that is given to you for free by someone else or by KwoxStudio. It is not a subscription and it will not be renewed automatically. And for the VIP you had before the gifted, when the gifted VIP is over, your previous VIP will be restored. The time you loosed with your VIP because of the gifted will be compensated.

VIP benefits

The VIP benefits are different in each game of the franchise. But the idea is the same. The VIP benefits are designed to enhance your experience in the game and give you access to exclusive skins and content that are not available to non-VIP players. The VIP don't affect the gameplay and don't give you any advantages.
What kinds of tools you could have with the VIP:
- Beautiful skins, and tags
- Play with more peoples and friends
- Early access to new features
- Sandbox access
- Creating skins / mods
- x next bought items with cost reduce
- Gain game money per month/week - And maybe more!
